WHERE WE STOPPED
----------------
The first deployment fails with "FUNCTION_INVOCATION_FAILED".
Cause (from the Vercel logs): "sqlite3.OperationalError: unable to open database file".
The app could not see DATABASE_URL, so the environment variables did not reach the
Production deployment. Fix: Part A and Part B below.

.env.vercel already has the right APP_URL:
APP_URL=https://task-manager-opal-xi-63.vercel.app


PART A - Add the settings (about 5 minutes)
--------------------------------------------
1. In Vercel open the project "task-manager" and click Settings (top).
2. In the left menu click Environment Variables.
3. Look at the list:
   - If variables such as DATABASE_URL are listed, check they are ticked for Production.
     If not, edit each one, or delete them all and do step 4.
   - If the list is empty, do step 4.
4. Add them all at once:
   - Open Task_Manager\.env.vercel in your editor, press Ctrl + A, then Ctrl + C.
   - In Vercel click into the first Key box and press Ctrl + V. Vercel fills in all rows.
   - Make sure Environments includes Production (ticking all three is fine).
   - Click Save.
5. You should see about 19 variables, including DATABASE_URL, JWT_SECRET and APP_URL.


PART B - Rebuild with the new settings
--------------------------------------
Settings only apply to new builds.
1. Click Deployments (top).
2. On the top deployment click the "..." menu on the right, then Redeploy, and confirm.
3. Wait until it shows Ready (a few minutes).
4. Open https://task-manager-opal-xi-63.vercel.app - the sign-in page should appear.
5. Tell Claude "done" in the next session; it checks the live site and continues.
